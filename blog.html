Mathematics

1. How to Calculate Percentage Easily

Percentage means a number expressed out of 100. It helps us understand marks, discounts, growth and many everyday calculations.

Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Total) × 100

Example

If you score 80 marks out of 100:

(80 ÷ 100) × 100 = 80%

You scored 80 percent.

Finance Basics

3. GST Calculation Explained

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to many goods and services in India.

Basic formula

GST Amount = Taxable Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

Example

For a taxable amount of ₹1,000 at an illustrative GST rate of 18%:

GST = ₹1,000 × 18 ÷ 100 = ₹180

Total = ₹1,180. Actual GST rates depend on the product or service.

Measurement

4. How to Convert Units

Unit conversion changes a measurement from one unit to another without changing the actual quantity.

Example

1 kilometre = 1,000 metres.

So, 5 kilometres = 5 × 1,000 = 5,000 metres.

Always use the correct conversion factor for the units you are converting.

5. How to Calculate Date Difference

Date difference tells you how much time lies between two calendar dates.

Example

If one date is 1 January and another is 11 January in the same year, the elapsed difference is 10 days.
